Matching Methodology
Pass 1 — Exact match: same date, same amount (±$0.02). 378 pairs cleared automatically.

Pass 2 — Timing difference: same amount, date within a 6-day window. Catches checks and deposits that post to the bank a few days after the GL entry. 12 pairs.

Pass 3 — Amount variance: same date (±1 day), amount within $100. Flags likely data-entry errors for manual review rather than auto-clearing them. 8 pairs, $20.74 net variance.

Unmatched: genuine reconciling items — outstanding checks, deposits in transit, and bank-only items (fees, interest, NSF) not yet booked to the GL.
